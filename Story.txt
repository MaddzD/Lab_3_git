The quiet didn’t drop all at once; it settled like dust over the shattered remains of the Interstate.

Mara adjusted the grip on her steel wrench, her breathing shallow behind a dirt-streaked bandana. Below the overpass, the highway was a river of rust and static—dozens of rusted sedans bumper-to-bumper, and between them, the wanderers.

They weren't the fast, screaming monsters from old midnight movies. These were relentless in their silence. Dragged feet. Jawbones clicking against dry throats. A slow, agonizing tide of sgrey skin and hollow eyes, driven by nothing more than the residual muscle memory of a world that had died three winters ago.

Beside her, Eli nudged a loose piece of concrete. It chipped off the edge, dropping sixty feet before landing with a sharp clack against a hood.

Down below, fifty heads snapped upward in eerie unison. No growls. Just the dry, synchronized rasp of a hundred heels turning on gravel.

"Careful," Mara whispered, pulling him back into the shadow of the overturned semi-truck. "They don't see well, but they track the sound like moths to a porch light."

Eli nodded, his hands shaking around a canvas sack filled with hardtack and canned beans—the day's bitter prize from an abandoned gas station two miles back. "How many?"

"Too many to fight. We wait until twilight."

They crouched in the shade, watching the swarm mill around the sound's origin. One of the infected, wearing the shredded remnants of a business suit, shuffled past a driver-side mirror, staring straight through its own reflection without a flicker of recognition. That was the terror of it. It wasn't hatred in those milk-white eyes; it was complete, absolute empty vacancy.

When the sun finally dipped, painting the horizon in bruised shades of purple, the heat bled out of the asphalt. The horde began to drift east, drawn by some distant, unseen instinct. Mara stood up slowly, her joints popping. The world belonged to the dead now, but as long as the sun kept setting and rising, the living would keep stealing it back, one quiet step at a time.
